Swimlane · brand ⇄ platform ⇄ creator

End-to-end campaign journey

The happy path from brief to review, with the two loops that matter: counter-offers during negotiation and revisions during content review. Creators can also enter at step 3 by applying to a public campaign or accepting an invite.

Step 1

Three ways to brief

Voice (Groq Whisper STT), PDF (parsed in the browser), or free text. All three go through brief analysis (Groq, Gemini fallback) to pre-fill the campaign form.

Step 4

Turn-based negotiation

Each offer or counter is a negotiation_turns row; the drawer polls every 4 s. Either party can accept the other's latest turn.

Admin

Off the main path

Admins see platform stats, manage users, moderate campaigns and delete reviews. They never enter the deal flow.
