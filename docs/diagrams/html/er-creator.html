Data model: identity and creator supply

Clerk owns authentication; users mirrors it by clerk_id and carries the role. The creator side stores everything the matching engine reads: platform stats, prices, niches and past brand work. See the campaign ER diagram for the demand side.

Provenance

data_source on social profiles

YouTube enrichment writes into creator_social_profiles and labels the row with data_source and is_api_verified, so API-verified stats are distinguishable from self-reported ones.

Not shown

Omitted tables

creator_languages, creator_portfolio_items, and the brand-side campaign tables.

Soft delete

deleted_at

Profiles and users are soft-deleted. Matching skips any creator with deleted_at set or is_available false.
