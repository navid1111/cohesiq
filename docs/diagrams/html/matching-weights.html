Matching score weights

The single source of truth is SCORE_WEIGHTS in backend/app/services/matching_config.py. Each sub-score is normalised to 0–1 and stored on ai_match_scores alongside score_semantic and score_total. Older docs that say 0.35 / 0.30 are superseded.

Why

A big creator can't outrank a fitting one

Engagement and reach together are worth 0.10. A correctly priced niche creator beats a massive off-niche one.

Caps

Semantic rescue is bounded

When the exact niche score is 0, semantic similarity (≥ 0.28) can stand in, capped at 0.40, so the most it adds to score_total is 0.18.

Unknowns

Missing data isn't free

Unknown budget scores 0.5; unknown recency scores 0.2. A missing signal ranks below a verified one.
